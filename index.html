About Us

We are a small, passionate team of journalists, writers, and truth-seekers who launched FAKTO News with a single mission: to deliver honest, verified news to readers who deserve better than the noise of modern media. In a world saturated with clickbait headlines and manufactured outrage, we believe there is still room — and desperate need — for a publication that puts facts above everything else.

"Journalism is printing what someone else does not want printed. Everything else is public relations."

Our Independence

FAKTO News stands entirely independent. We do not align ourselves with any political party, government, ethnic group, religious institution, or corporate interest. Our loyalty is to the truth and to our readers — nothing more, nothing less. Whether the story is popular or uncomfortable, our commitment remains the same: report what is real, verify what is claimed, and let the readers form their own conclusions.

Our Values

Independence is not just a word to us — it is the foundation upon which everything we do is built. Every article you read on FAKTO News has been carefully researched, cross-verified, and edited with the singular purpose of informing you truthfully.

Our Approach

At FAKTO News, we cover a wide range of topics — from world affairs and politics to technology, business, and cultural moments that shape our times. Regardless of the subject, our methodology remains consistent: we investigate thoroughly, source rigorously, write clearly, and correct openly when we make mistakes.

We also believe that news should be accessible. That's why we've designed FAKTO to be clean, minimal, and free of intrusive advertisements. When you visit us, you visit for the stories — not for the pop-ups.

Join Us

We are just getting started, and we invite you to be part of this movement toward better, more honest journalism. Read our stories, share what resonates with you, question what challenges you, and hold us accountable to the standards we've set for ourselves.

— The FAKTO News Team
