About FaktoNews

We are a small, passionate team of journalists, writers, and truth-seekers who launched FAKTO News with a single mission: to deliver honest, verified news to readers who deserve better than the noise of modern media.

FAKTO News stands entirely independent. We do not align ourselves with any political party, government, or corporate interest. Our loyalty is to the truth and to our readers.

Independence is not just a word to us — it is the foundation upon which everything we do is built. Every article you read on FAKTO News has been carefully researched and edited with the singular purpose of informing you truthfully.
